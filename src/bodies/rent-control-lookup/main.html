About NJ Rent Control

New Jersey does not have a statewide rent control law. However, municipalities are permitted to enact their own rent control ordinances, and many New Jersey municipalities have — each with its own caps, exemptions, and administrative requirements.

If your property is in a rent-controlled municipality, you must comply with local rules before increasing rent. Failure to register can eliminate your ability to collect increases.
