Frequently asked questions

How do I pay rent?

Log in to the tenant portal and navigate to Payments. You can pay via ACH bank transfer (no fee) or credit/debit card. Autopay is available.

What counts as an emergency maintenance request?

Emergencies include no heat in winter, active water leaks or flooding, gas smell, fire, total electrical failure, or a lockout. Call {{phone.display}} immediately and also submit a portal request for documentation.

How do I give notice to vacate?

Check your lease for the required notice period (typically 60 days before lease end). Submit written notice via email to {{email}} and through your tenant portal. We'll confirm receipt and schedule a move-out inspection.

When will I get my security deposit back?

Under NJ law, your security deposit must be returned within 30 days of move-out, along with an itemized statement of any deductions. Deductions are limited to unpaid rent and actual damage beyond normal wear and tear.

Who do I contact for a question about my lease?

Email {{email}} or call {{phone.display}} during business hours. Your property manager will respond within one business day.
