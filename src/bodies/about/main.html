Local NJ property management, built differently

We started PPM because NJ landlords deserved better

There are two kinds of property management companies in New Jersey.

The first is the large franchise operation. They have a name you recognize and a call center you'll get routed through every time you have a question about your own property.

The second is the solo operator — a landlord who started managing a few other people's rentals on the side, figuring things out as they go.

Proactive Property Management was founded in 2023 to be a third option. Professionally run. Locally rooted. Small enough to know your property by name.

Who we are

Dan Rivera, founder and principal

B.S., Cornell University

Dan holds a B.S. from Cornell University and has spent over a decade active in New Jersey real estate — as an investor, a licensed agent, and a property manager. He's been on both sides of the landlord-tenant relationship and built PPM with that perspective: if you're going to hand someone the keys to your investment, they should treat it the way they'd treat their own.

We charge 10% of the rent we collect, never more than $275 per unit a month and at least $150, plus a one-time onboarding fee and a fee for each new lease or renewal. Every fee is on our pricing page. No hidden fees, no technology fees, no maintenance markups.

How we work

  • One point of contact

    You're not calling a general line. You have a direct number for the person who knows your property.

  • New Jersey compliance, built in

    New Jersey landlord-tenant law sets specific security deposit rules and good-cause eviction requirements, and rent control is set town by town. We handle the compliance work.

  • What you see is what you pay

    One monthly management fee on rent collected, an onboarding fee deducted from your first payout, and a fee for each new lease or renewal, all published up front. No technology fees, no markup on contractor invoices.
